GG Engage is committed to protecting your privacy.  This Privacy Statement

1. NO DATA COLLECTED
   This Software does NOT collect, transmit, upload, or share any personal
   data, photos, metadata, or usage information with the Licensor, any third
   party, or any remote server.

   All processing occurs entirely on your local computer.

2. LOCAL STORAGE ONLY
   The Software stores the following data ONLY on your own computer, in the
   folder  %APPDATA%\GGEngagePhotoProcessor\:

   - The number of photos you have processed (for the free-trial counter).
   - Your licence key (if you have purchased one), stored in a plain
     JSON file accessible only to your Windows user account.

   This data never leaves your computer.

3. THIRD-PARTY REVERSE GEOCODING (NOMINATIM)
   When the Software processes a geotagged photo, it sends the GPS
   coordinates (latitude and longitude only) to the Nominatim geocoding
   service operated by OpenStreetMap Foundation (openstreetmap.org) in order
   to look up the place name for that location.

   - No photo content, personal details, or identifying information is sent.
   - Only the GPS coordinates extracted from the photo's EXIF data are
     transmitted.
   - Nominatim's usage policy and privacy information can be found at
     https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/
   - Requests are rate-limited to one per second in accordance with that
     policy.
   - If you do not wish GPS coordinates to be sent to Nominatim, do not
     process geotagged photos with this Software.

4. NO ANALYTICS, ADVERTISING, OR TRACKING
   The Software contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting
   service, or any form of tracking technology.

5. CHILDREN
