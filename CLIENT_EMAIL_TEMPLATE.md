# How to Request a Social Media Post

Send an email to **[your monitored email address]** using the template below.
The system will automatically generate your post within 2 minutes.

---

## Email Template

**Subject:** [WGPOST] Your Product or Offer Name

**Body:**

```
PRODUCT: [Name of the product, service, or offer]

DESCRIPTION: [2-3 sentences describing it — what it is, what problem it solves, who it's for]

OFFER: [Any special deal, discount, or limited-time offer — leave blank if none]

TARGET: [Who is this for? e.g. "small business owners", "tradies", "parents"]

WEBSITE: [Link to more info — leave blank to use the Web Gecko referral link]
```

Attach a photo to the email and it will be used in the post.
If no photo is attached, a branded Web Gecko card will be created automatically.

---

## Example

**Subject:** [WGPOST] Summer Website Sale — 50% Off

**Body:**

```
PRODUCT: Summer Starter Website Package

DESCRIPTION: A professional 5-page website built with AI, delivered in 48 hours.
Includes contact form, Google Maps, and mobile-friendly design.
Perfect for small businesses that need to get online fast.

OFFER: 50% off for the first month — normally $499, now $249. Limited to 10 spots.

TARGET: Small business owners and sole traders in Australia

WEBSITE: https://webgecko.com.au/summer-sale
```

---

## Tips

- Always include `[WGPOST]` in the subject line — this is what triggers the automation
- The more detail you give in DESCRIPTION, the better the generated post
- JPG or PNG photos work best (under 5MB)
- You'll receive a notification once the post is reviewed and published
